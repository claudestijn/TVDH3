Possessions (counted) follow from the order of actions: a possession ends with a made shot, a made last free throw, a turnover or an offensive foul; after a miss the rebound decides. Possessions (formula) = field goal attempts + 0.44 × free throw attempts − off. rebounds + turnovers, for comparison. eFG% = (2PT made + 1.5 × 3PT made) / field goal attempts. True shooting % = points / (2 × (field goal attempts + 0.44 × free throw attempts)).
